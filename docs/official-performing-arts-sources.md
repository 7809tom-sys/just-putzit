# Official Performing-Arts Source Findings

## UW–Milwaukee Peck School of the Arts

- **Official calendar:** `https://uwm.edu/arts/events-community/performing-arts/`
- The public page exposes upcoming performing-arts titles, date/time text, and a link to its full official arts events calendar at `https://uwm.edu/arts/events/`.
- It is suitable as an official Milwaukee campus source for theatre, music, and dance events. The existing UW–Milwaukee general campus source remains available; this dedicated source adds more precise arts coverage.

## Carroll University

- **Official campus calendar:** `https://carrollu.campusgroups.com/events`
- The calendar is public and includes a dedicated **Carroll Players** organization filter, alongside visual and performing-arts categories.
- The public list relies on dynamic filtering and may need a targeted organization/calendar endpoint before a production scraper is added. It is not being added until a stable public listing shape is confirmed.
