# Verified Madison Public Venue Sources

## City of Madison Goodman Pool

- Official source: https://www.cityofmadison.com/parks/pool
- The City of Madison Parks Division operates the Irwin A. and Robert D. Goodman Pool at **325 W. Olin Avenue, Madison, WI 53715**.
- The official 2026 page lists the season as June 11 through August 23 and states that visitors age three and older must complete a pool waiver.
- Discovery use: a city-operated public swimming-pool venue. Members should still confirm seasonal hours, admission, and waiver requirements on the official page.

## Warner Park Community Recreation Center

- Official source: https://www.cityofmadison.com/parks/wpcrc
- The City of Madison Parks Division lists Warner Park Community Recreation Center at **1625 Northport Drive, Madison, WI 53704**.
- The official page describes two gymnasiums, a fitness center, pickleball courts, studio space, community rooms, programs, and events.
- Discovery use: a city-operated community recreation venue for public gym, court sports, pickleball, fitness, and community activities. Members should confirm eligibility, hours, and reservation requirements with the facility.
