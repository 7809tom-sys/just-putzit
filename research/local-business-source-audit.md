# Verified Waukesha Local Business Sources

## Café de Arts Roastery

The business’s official website states that Café de Arts is a family-owned and operated European coffee house and micro-roastery established in 2009, located in Waukesha. Its official listing provides the address **830 W. St. Paul Ave., Waukesha, WI**, business hours, phone number, and menu/coffee handoffs. Source: <https://cafedearts.com/>.

## Waukesha Cafe House

The business’s official website identifies Waukesha Cafe House as a small business in downtown Waukesha, founded by Mojib and Narmin Rasuli in 2018 and opened in 2023. It lists **410 Wisconsin Avenue, Waukesha, WI 53186**, current daily hours, phone details, and official menu/visit information. Source: <https://www.waukeshacafehouse.com/>.

## Tally’s Tap & Eatery

The business’s official website describes Tally’s Tap & Eatery as a third-generation family-owned and operated Waukesha restaurant and bar since 2013. It lists **S31W24661 West Sunset Drive, Waukesha, WI 53189**, current hours, a menu, and an official online-ordering handoff. Source: <https://tallystap.com/>.

## Copilot Coffee Co.

Copilot Coffee Co.’s official website confirms its Waukesha location at **279 W Main St., Waukesha, WI**, current hours, and breakfast/lunch service. The site does not make an ownership claim in the reviewed content, so it may be shown as a local Waukesha coffee option but should not receive a “locally owned” badge without further verified evidence. Source: <https://www.copilotcoffeeco.com/>.
