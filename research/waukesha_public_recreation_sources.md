# Waukesha Public Recreation Sources

## Official Sources

- City of Waukesha Pools: https://www.waukesha-wi.gov/government/departments/pools.php
  - Buchner Park Community Pool, 223 Oakland Avenue, Waukesha, WI 53186.
  - Horeb Springs Aquatic Center, 330 Spring Street, Waukesha, WI 53188.
  - Official page lists 2026 pool seasons, open swim hours, daily admission, lap/current-channel access, lessons, and facility features. Non-residents have daily admission pricing; member-only windows are explicitly identified.

- Waukesha County Fox Brook Park: https://www.waukeshacounty.gov/parks-and-land-use/parks-and-recreation/parks-and-trails/fox-brook-park/
  - 2925 N Barker Rd, Brookfield, WI 53045.
  - Public swimming beach, volleyball, paved trail, kayak rental, wildlife viewing, and other amenities. Park access requires an annual membership or daily vehicle permit; beach is open sunrise–10 p.m. in season.

- Waukesha County Beaches: https://www.waukeshacounty.gov/parks-and-land-use/parks-and-recreation/parks-and-trails/beaches/
  - Six public swimming beaches: Fox Brook Park, Menomonee Park, Minooka Park (1927 E Sunset Dr, Waukesha, WI 53189), Mukwonago Park, Muskego Park, and Naga-Waukee Park.
  - Beaches are swim-at-own-risk with no lifeguards, designated swim areas, seasonal dates, water-quality advisories, and a required park annual membership or daily permit.

- YMCA of Greater Waukesha County: https://www.gwcymca.org/
  - Public-facing membership and program source covering fitness, swim, sports and recreation, group exercise, aquatic exercise, sports leagues, seasonal family events, and community involvement. Guest/member eligibility and registration vary by program.

## Implementation Notes

Waukesha-specific curated activities should use city `Waukesha`, state `WI`, official URLs, and access notes that distinguish daily admission, permits, membership, seasonal schedules, and water-quality advisories. Brookfield, Menomonee Falls, Hartland, and other Waukesha County communities should remain separate city records so GPS radius filtering can include them by distance without relabeling them as Waukesha city.
