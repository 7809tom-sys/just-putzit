# Public Recreation Discovery Sources

Research recorded August 11, 2026 for the Milwaukee-area launch market.

| Public-access activity | Official source | Verified details used in discovery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Outdoor pools | https://county.milwaukee.gov/EN/Parks/Explore/Pools/Outdoor | Wilson Pool has lap, open, and night swim; Sheridan Pool offers open and night swim. Seasonal schedules and admissions should be confirmed on the source before attendance. |
| Beaches | https://county.milwaukee.gov/EN/Parks/Explore/Lakefront/Beaches | Bradford, Grant, and McKinley beaches provide public lakefront access; swimming is at visitors' risk and hours/advisories may change. |
| Community centers and basketball | https://county.milwaukee.gov/EN/Parks/Explore/Community-Centers and https://mkecoparks.helpscoutdocs.com/article/359-where-can-i-play-basketball | Milwaukee County lists King and Kosciuszko community centers and outdoor basketball courts. Daylight courts are generally first-come, first-served; confirm schedules and permits. |
| YMCA | https://www.ymcamke.org/locations/rite-hite-family-ymca/ | Rite-Hite Family YMCA provides fitness equipment and an indoor running/walking track; programs and access terms should be confirmed with the YMCA. |
| Golf | https://mke.golf/ | Milwaukee County Parks operates 13 public golf courses and publishes current course conditions and tee-time access. |
| Disc golf | https://county.milwaukee.gov/EN/Parks/Experience/Sports/Disc-Golf | Milwaukee County lists six full disc-golf courses, five free practice courses, day/annual permits, and course-hour rules. |
| Tennis and volleyball | https://county.milwaukee.gov/EN/Parks/Experience/Get-Active and https://county.milwaukee.gov/EN/Parks/Plan/Rent-an-Athletic-Field | County facilities support tennis, beach volleyball, and other court sports; many courts have free first-come use when unreserved. |
| Dirt-track and OHM riding | https://romewi.gov/dyracuse/ and https://dnr.wisconsin.gov/topic/parks/recreation/ohm | Dyracuse in the Town of Rome has public day-pass dirt tracks and OHM/ATV trails. It is a regional trip, so the app should show access terms and distance/city context clearly. |
| City recreation programs | https://www.milwaukeerecreation.net/ | Milwaukee Recreation offers indoor pools, playfields, sports, wellness, and community programming; schedules should be sourced dynamically from the official calendar where possible. |
